## Top 5 do-next

1. **Stop trusting the client for money math (H1 + H2).** Recompute `billableUnits` server-side in `submitHrmSessionEmrNote` and fix/unify the CRM 8-minute table (>97 min undercount). Two small edits + boundary tests; every downstream unit surface (weekly grid, auth burn-down, payroll, Plutus) inherits the fix.
2. **Make Studio submit atomic and idempotent (H4 + H3 + M2).** One `$transaction` around session→note→trials→behavior-logs with delete-before-reinsert kills the resubmit double-count, the partial-write orphan states, and (with prefetch) the per-item N+1 in the same PR.
3. **Guard the sign/convert/deficiency state machine (H6 + H7 + M10).** Add `bcbaSigned: false`/`isConverted: false` conditions to the three write sites so re-entry can't overwrite signer identity, convert twice, or strip signatures off a converted claim.
4. **Pin `America/New_York` in the three billing-window helpers (H5).** `startOfWeekMonday`/`localDateKey` in `weeklyBillableUnits.ts` and `inWindow` in `authUnits.ts` are the only functions that need `date-fns-tz` for week and auth boundaries to stop depending on host TZ.
5. **Slim the client profile query (H9) and whitelist `approveDocument` (H8).** The single worst PHI-over-the-wire page and the single worst unvalidated-input action; both are contained, well-understood edits.
